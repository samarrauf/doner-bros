Our story

Two brothers, one grill, zero shortcuts.

Doner Bros started with a simple idea: a doner should be something you crave, not something you settle for. We marinate our meat overnight, stack it by hand every morning and slow-roast it on the vertical grill until it's perfectly crisp.

Our bread is baked fresh throughout the day, our salads are chopped to order and every sauce is made from our own family recipes.

  • Marinated overnight Spices and yoghurt for tender, full-flavoured meat.
  • Fresh-baked bread Soft, warm and made in-house all day.
  • House-made sauces Garlic, chilli and herb, all made from scratch.
