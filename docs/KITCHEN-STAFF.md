# Kitchen board: how to use it

The kitchen board shows every open order and rings when a new one arrives.

## At the start of every shift

1. Open **doner-bros.vercel.app/kitchen/login**, type the kitchen password and tap **Log in**.
   You stay logged in for 14 hours. If you forget the password, ask the owner to reset it.
2. Tap the big **START KITCHEN** button. You will hear a short chime: that means sound works.
3. Leave the board open all day.

If the board ever shows **"Sound is paused"**, tap the button again.

## Set up the device once

- **Use Chrome.** Open the board, tap the lock icon next to the address, open **Site settings**
  and set **Sound** to **Allow**. Then the board can ring even after a restart.
- **Pin the tab** so it doesn't get closed by mistake (tap and hold the tab, then **Pin**).
- **Keep it plugged in** to the charger.
- **Never let it sleep:** in the device's Display settings, set **Screen timeout / Sleep** to
  **Never** (or the longest time).
- **Keep the volume up** and make sure the device is not on silent or Do Not Disturb.

## Using the board

- New orders appear in **New**, ring for about 3 seconds and flash.
- An order left in **New** for more than 2 minutes pings once a minute until someone starts it.
- Tap the big button on a card to move it on: **Start → Ready → Complete**.
- **◀ Back** moves an order one step back. **✕ Cancel** cancels it. Both ask you to confirm.
- Tap the phone number on a card to call the customer.
- The 🔔 icon at the top turns sound off (🔕) and on again.
